Behind the Lines: Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Behind the Lines is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android, made for family and friends. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. It never connects to the internet: every level and picture is built into the app. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (which levels you've solved, and your hints) on your phone so you can carry on where you left off. If your phone's own backup is turned on (iCloud on iPhone, Google's backup on Android), that progress is included in your backup like any other app's. That backup is between you and Apple or Google; we can't see it.

The game also keeps a log of taps on the board, used to tune how the game feels. It's a file on your phone (in the Files app on iPhone, or in Downloads on Android). It records only which level you were playing, where on the board your finger landed and when, never anything about you, and it only leaves your phone if you choose to share it.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
